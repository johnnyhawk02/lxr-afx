VT323 font (loaded from Google Fonts)  -  SIL Open Font License 1.1, by Peter Hull.

Textures: none downloaded. The 3D panel's wear (rubbed print, fine scratches, smudges), its satin/brushed
grain and the wooden desk are drawn by app/view3d.js at load time from a fixed random seed, so there is no
image file to credit. If CC0 texture files are added later (e.g. from ambientCG or Poly Haven), list each one
here with its URL.

Knob model: the knobs are turned on a lathe in app/view3d.js. An STL of your own can be loaded in LOOK
(kept in that browser), or put in the repo and named in KNOB_STL at the top of app/view3d.js.
